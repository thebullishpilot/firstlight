Privacy Policy

First Light: Morning Ritual · Effective October 12, 2026

In short: First Light does not collect, store or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

First Light is published by Heart and Soil Living LTD. Questions about this policy: harmvadev@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your completed sessions, your days in a row, the intention you write for the day and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. The date the app was first opened is also kept on the device, to count the free trial. Deleting the app deletes your sessions, intention and settings.

Sound

All sound is generated on your device. The app does not use the microphone, the camera or your location.

Subscriptions

The subscription is sold and billed by Apple. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple, on your device, whether a subscription is active. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone. It is not sent from a server, and we do not know whether you have it on.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate servers for this app, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
