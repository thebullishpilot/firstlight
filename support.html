Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?
First Light is free for three days from the first time you open it. After that, a monthly subscription keeps it going. You are not charged anything unless you choose to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then First Light. You can also get there from Settings inside the app, under Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I subscribed but the app still asks me to subscribe.
Tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen or in the app's Settings. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

I can't hear anything.
Turn the volume up with the side buttons while a session is playing. The sound starts quietly and fades in over the first few seconds. The lowest soundscape, Still Water, is fullest on headphones.

Does the sound keep playing when I lock my phone?
Yes. You can pause and resume from the lock screen.

Why didn't my day count?
A day counts when a session plays all the way to the closing bells. Ending a session early does not count.

Does the app work without a connection?
Yes. Sessions need no connection. Only subscribing or restoring a purchase needs one.
