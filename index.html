First Light

A few quiet minutes to start the day clear

First Light is a morning ritual for iPhone. Sit down with your coffee, press Begin, and a few minutes of generated sound and slow, paced breathing carry you from waking up to focused.

  • Sessions of 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes
  • Sound that rises and falls with your breath, so you can follow it with your eyes closed
  • Four stages: Arrive, Breathe, Focus, Begin
  • Three soundscapes: Porch, Clear Sky and Still Water
  • Set one intention for the day and keep count of your mornings in a row
  • No account and no data collection

First Light is a relaxation and focus practice. It is not a medical device and does not treat any condition.
